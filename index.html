Private finance workspace

Mimo Finance Command Center

A private, offline finance app in development for Android and Windows. Record income and expenses, organize accounts and review monthly totals. The native app stores the ledger in an encrypted database on your device and does not require a Google Account for offline use.

Privacy policy

Planned Google integrations

Gmail would help review finance correspondence and prepare invoice drafts. Google Calendar would show due dates, Google Drive is being considered for encrypted backups, and Google Sheets would support migrating existing records. These integrations are not active in the current native app. This website does not connect to your Google Account.
