Scope and current status

This policy covers Mimo Finance Command Center, a private, single-owner finance app in development for Android and Windows. It does not describe unrelated services on this website. This page does not connect to Google, request account permissions, or collect finance records.

The current native app works offline. It stores account details and income or expense entries you enter, including categories, amounts, currencies, dates and any exchange-rate information needed for reporting. It uses these records to maintain your ledger and show monthly totals and recent entries. You do not need a Google Account for these features.

The current native app does not access Google user data. Earlier prototypes included Gmail search and draft creation and a Sheets-backed workflow; those prototypes are not part of the current native app. Live Google access and cloud sync have not been implemented in the native app.

Google services and permissions

These are planned uses, not active integrations. If a feature is enabled, connecting it will be optional. The app will explain why it needs access and request permission for that feature through Google. You can decline and continue using the offline ledger. The app will not ask you to provide your Google password to Mimo.

Gmail

Data and access: Read relevant messages, including sender, subject, date and message content needed to review finance-related correspondence. If invoice drafting is enabled, create Gmail drafts with the entered recipients, subject and body.

Purpose: Identify receipts and payment notices for review and prepare invoice email drafts. Creating a draft would not send it.

Google Calendar

Data and access: Create and manage app-created payment or invoice due-date events, including event titles, dates, reminder settings and event IDs.

Purpose: Show due dates and reminders. This does not require reading unrelated calendars or events.

Google Drive

Data and access: If Drive is selected for backup, upload and retrieve app-created finance backup files and the file identifiers or metadata needed to find them.

Purpose: Back up and restore finance records. Backup files are intended to be encrypted on the device before upload. General Drive browsing is not a planned use.

Google Sheets

Data and access: Read data from a spreadsheet you designate for migration, including spreadsheet and tab identifiers and relevant cells such as dates, descriptions, amounts and currencies.

Purpose: Import and reconcile existing finance records. The planned migration would leave the source spreadsheet unchanged.

Storage and protection

The native ledger is stored on your device in a SQLCipher-encrypted database. Its encryption key is kept separately using platform secure storage. The current native app does not upload the ledger to a Mimo server or sync it to Google Drive.

If Google features are enabled, authorization and API requests will use Google’s HTTPS endpoints. Any access or refresh credentials kept on the device are to be stored in platform secure storage, separate from the ledger encryption key. Finance records imported from Google and saved to the ledger are to be protected by the same local database encryption. Drafts and events created in Google remain in the relevant Google service; any future Drive backup would be stored in your Google account.

Sharing and Google Limited Use

If Google integrations are enabled, Mimo Finance Command Center’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This applies to original and derived data. Google user data will be used only to provide or improve the disclosed features you use in the app. It will not be sold, used for advertising, or sent to AI providers or used to train AI models.

Google processes requests for features you authorize. Other transfers of Google user data are limited to those permitted by the applicable policy, such as transfers needed for a disclosed feature with your consent, for security or legal compliance, or as part of a qualifying business transfer with explicit consent. People will not read Google user data except as permitted by the policy, such as with specific consent, for security or legal reasons, or when the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Local ledger records remain on your device until you remove the local data. If Google features are enabled, Google data saved as part of a ledger record would remain with that record until removed. Authorization credentials are kept only as needed to maintain a connection. Copies in your Google account or in backups you control remain there until you remove them, subject to the service’s retention settings.

  1. Revoke Google access. Open Google Account connections, select Mimo Finance Command Center if it is listed, and choose the option to remove its access. Revocation stops future access under that permission; it does not erase previously saved records, drafts, events or backups.
  2. Remove copies you control. To remove the entire local ledger, clear the app’s storage on Android or remove its local app data on Windows. This also removes finance records entered manually. Delete any separate backup copies you no longer want. If you later create Gmail drafts, Calendar events or Drive backups with the app, remove them in the relevant Google service. Removing an imported copy does not delete the original Gmail message or spreadsheet.
  3. Request deletion. Email a data deletion request and identify the data you want removed. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or copies of financial records. We can help identify where the data is stored and address copies under our control, where applicable. We cannot remotely erase data on your device or in your Google account.
